Claude also disallowed duplicate check-ins with a unique constraint for QR code uploads. In addition, Claude added status fields (pending, verified, rejected) and status_message (“QR code not readable”). The added field processed_at records when the upload was checked. This overall made the Lecture and QRCodeUpload tables more complete than my original tables.
